How a Billericay booking works

Most Billericay bookings start with a phone call or a message, not with the computer already on the bench. Tell us whether you are in the town itself, Stock, Little Burstead, Great Burstead or Queens Park, and whether you can bring the machine to Wickford. On the call we ask what the computer is doing, how long it has been doing it, and whether a drink, a drop or a loose hinge is involved. We also ask if the files matter more than getting the machine running. That decides whether a screen or battery job can go ahead as a repair, or whether we should talk about the files first. We do not need a technical diagnosis from you. A plain description is enough.

Drop-off is at 32 Glebe Road, Wickford, Essex SS11 8EU, and it is by appointment. Call 07367 652987 and we will agree a time so the bench is free. Please bring the charger if the fault is power, battery or charging. If the lid is cracked, leave shattered glass alone and bring the machine as it is. We would rather see the original damage than a lid that has been peeled apart at home.

At the workshop the diagnostic is free. We tell you what we found, in plain English, and the fixed quote. Work starts after you approve it. For a repair, there is no labour charge if we cannot fix it, a part is not worth fitting, or the job would cost more than the computer is worth. That applies to repairs. When the job is a screen, a charging port or a battery, it typically takes 1 to 3 working days once the part is in, and the new part fitted has a 12-month warranty. A hinge, a keyboard, a dust clean or a software job is timed in the quote. We will say if a Billericay collection needs to wait until the machine is ready.

If you are comparing a repair with a new laptop, bring that up when we quote. We will say whether the quoted repair is a sensible spend for the computer you have. We will not invent a success rate, and we will not tell you a machine will last until a particular year.
