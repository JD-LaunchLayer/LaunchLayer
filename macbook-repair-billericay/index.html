MacBook Repair FAQ — Billericay

My MacBook is a 2015-2017 model — is it still worth repairing?

Usually yes. Apple may list older models as "vintage" or "obsolete" for its own repair programme, but that doesn't mean the hardware is unrepairable — it just means Apple has stopped offering it. We can still source parts and repair most faults on these models.

Do you collect from Billericay village areas, or just the town centre?

All of it — free collection covers the full CM11/CM12 area, including Stock, Little Burstead, Great Burstead, and Queens Park, not just the town centre.

What parts do you actually use?

Genuine "pulled" parts recovered from donor MacBooks, or strict OEM-equivalent components when a pulled part isn't available. We'll always tell you which before starting any work.
