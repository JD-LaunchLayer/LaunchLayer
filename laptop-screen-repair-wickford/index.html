Signs the screen needs replacing

These are the faults we see most often on the bench.

Cracked glass, picture still there

You can still read the screen through the crack. Don't press it. Extra pressure can push the crack into the panel underneath.

Dark patches and colour bleed

A black mark, an oily patch, or colour leaking from a corner means the panel is failing. It rarely stays the size it is today.

Lines, flicker, or a dim picture

Lines, a flicker, or a picture that has gone dim or pink can be the panel or the cable. We check which one it is before we quote a new screen.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about getting a screen replaced.

How much does a screen replacement cost?

Windows and Chromebook screen fitting is £50 plus the panel. MacBook screen fitting is £85 plus the panel, and Mac panels usually cost more. The quote is that labour plus the cost of the panel for your model. Diagnosis is free, you approve a fixed quote before any work starts, and prices include VAT.

How long will the repair take?

Most screen repairs take 1 to 3 working days. It mainly depends on how fast the right panel arrives.

Will I lose any personal data or files?

Replacing a screen does not normally require a drive wipe. Tell us if your files are the priority, and we'll discuss any data risks before work starts.

Is it worth repairing a cracked screen, or should I replace the laptop?

If the rest of the laptop is working, repairing the screen is usually the sensible choice: your files stay put, and you keep a laptop you already know. If the battery or another fault is part of it, we say so before a panel is ordered. Diagnosis is free, and you approve a fixed quote before any work starts.

Can you replace a touchscreen or a 2-in-1 panel?

Yes, when a matching panel is available for that model. A touchscreen, including the panel on a 2-in-1, is a different part from a plain laptop screen, so the panel price is not the same. Fitting is still £50 on a Windows laptop or Chromebook, or £85 on a MacBook, plus that panel. We quote the panel for your model before we order it.

My screen is cracked but it still works. Should I keep using it?

If the glass is still held in place and no dark patch is spreading, you can use it carefully for now. Don't press the cracked glass. If the glass is loose, or a black mark is spreading, close the lid and book it in. If you still need your files before then, plug the laptop into a TV or spare monitor. The files sit on the drive, not on the glass.
