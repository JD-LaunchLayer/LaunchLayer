Laptop Screen FAQ — Wickford

How much does a screen replacement cost?

Our lab fitting labor is set at a flat £50 for Windows/Chrome laptops and £65 for MacBooks. Your final quote is simply our flat labor rate plus the exact trade-invoice component price of your specific display panel.

How long will the repair take?

Most screen repairs take 1 to 3 days. The timeline is dictated entirely by how fast our trusted UK electronic component distributors can deliver your model's exact matching display panel to our Wickford workshop.

Will I lose any personal data or files?

No. Display matrix swaps are purely structural and external. Your storage sectors, local profiles, personal folders, and applications stay entirely safe on your untouched SSD while we work.
