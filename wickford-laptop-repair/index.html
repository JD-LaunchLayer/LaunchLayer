Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

How much does a laptop screen replacement cost?

Our expert fitting labour starts at £99. The total repair price is this labour fee plus the exact cost of the replacement display panel required for your specific model configuration. We always provide a clear, fixed quote before ordering any parts.

My laptop refuses to charge — can it be fixed?

Yes, in the vast majority of cases. This issue is typically caused by a degraded external power brick, an aging internal battery cell, or a physically damaged internal DC charging port connector. We will isolate the exact failure point during our free diagnostic scan.

Can you recover files if my laptop won't turn on?

Absolutely. Your operating system and files sit securely inside your internal hard drive or NVMe storage drive. Even if the main motherboard is completely dead, we can extract your family documents and photos safely from £79 and migrate them over to an external drive.

What exact areas around Wickford do you cover?
