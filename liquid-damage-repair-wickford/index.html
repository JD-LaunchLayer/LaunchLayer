Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Can I save my laptop by putting it in rice?

Unfortunately, rice is a widespread tech myth. While it might absorb visible surface wetness, it cannot stop active electrochemical corrosion spreading beneath tightly packed motherboard shields. Worse, rice leaves behind fine starch dust that blocks internal cooling fans and ports.

Does the motherboard repair tier include a new keyboard?

Our core pricing tiers focus strictly on repairing and cleaning the electronics on the motherboard itself. If liquid has damaged your physical keyboard keys or trackpad, we will always provide a separate, fair quote for the cost of the replacement part.

Will my photos, documents, and personal files be safe?

Yes. Motherboard cleaning and micro-soldering are highly precise procedures that do not modify your storage chips. To ensure absolute data security, our standard safety procedure is to completely isolate or safely extract your storage drive before starting deep liquid treatments.

How long does a professional liquid recovery take?

Proper liquid damage repair is a meticulous process. A typical workflow—including diagnostic microscopic tracing, ultrasonic chemical baths, multi-hour dehydration baking, circuit-level repair work, and extensive stability testing—usually takes between 1 to 3 business days.
