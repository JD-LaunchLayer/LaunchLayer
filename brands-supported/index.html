Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Do you use genuine Apple parts for MacBooks?

Apple heavily restricts access to brand-new OEM parts. To provide you with the best quality and value, we source genuine "pulled" parts from donor machines, or strictly OEM-equivalent displays that match the exact Retina resolution and colour grading of your original screen.

Will repairing my laptop void the manufacturer's warranty?

If your device is still under its original 1-year manufacturer warranty or an extended care plan (like AppleCare), we always advise taking it to them first. Independent physical repairs (like opening the chassis or replacing parts) generally void existing manufacturer warranties.

What if my specific brand isn't listed here?

The brands listed above make up 95% of our repairs, but we work on almost all Windows, ChromeOS, and macOS architecture. If you have a niche brand (like Razer, Gigabyte, or Panasonic), simply contact us. The internal components (Intel, AMD, Nvidia) are universal.

Do you repair custom-built gaming PCs?

Yes. Custom rigs are our specialty. Whether you built it yourself from Scan or Overclockers, or bought a pre-built system that is now crashing, we can diagnose specific component failures (Motherboard, GPU, PSU) and replace only what is broken.
