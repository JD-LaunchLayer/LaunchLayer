Computer & Laptop Repair Queries

Do you offer computer repairs near me?

Yes. We provide a completely Free Collection and Return Delivery service across Wickford, Basildon, Rayleigh, Billericay, Southend, and Brentwood. All primary technical diagnostics and complex component fixes are handled inside our centralised local workshop, meaning you skip the hassle of town traffic or high-street retail parking fees.

What specific repair services do you provide?

We handle everything from physical hardware faults to deep operating system conflicts, including:

  • Performance Boosts: Ultra-fast NVMe SSD configurations and memory (RAM) upgrades to fix slow laptops and lagging desktop towers.
  • Physical Repairs: Laptop display screen replacements, faulty charging port (DC Jack) fixes, battery replacements, keyboard swaps, and noise-reducing internal dust servicing.
  • Software Remediation: Virus eradication, malicious tracking cleanup, popup suppression, and clean Windows or macOS operating system profiles.
  • Bespoke Work: Precision component assembly for high-end custom gaming PCs and silent, reliable home office workstations.
How much does a typical workshop repair cost?

We believe in total transparency. Every ticket starts with a 100% free workshop diagnostic check, and standard software tune-ups or hardware repair paths start from £40 plus parts. Following your free diagnostic, we supply a strict, fixed-rate quote with zero hidden fees and zero surprise workshop additions so you know exactly what you are paying before we touch a single component.

What exactly does your "No-Fix, No-Fee" guarantee mean?

It is an absolute, risk-free guarantee. In the rare case that a device component is physically unrepairable, or if the necessary part sourcing options are simply not cost-effective for your machine's age, you do not owe us a single penny for our bench labour or diagnostic time.

How long will my computer repair take to complete?

We pride ourselves on rapid local turnarounds. Fault diagnosis is typically completed within 24 hours of landing on our bench, and the vast majority of standard hardware replacements, screen fixes, or software cleanups are fully executed, stress-tested, and returned within 1 to 3 working days.

Do you support Apple MacBooks and Mac desktops?

Yes. We offer professional out-of-warranty hardware fixes across all macOS devices—handling MacBook battery cell extractions, cracked Retina display replacements, trackpad faults, and sluggish iMac performance overhauls using premium components.

Will my files, documents, and photos remain safe?

Absolute privacy is our baseline standard. We use strict standalone workshop security protocols to isolate and protect your photo libraries, local accounting databases, and personal profile folders. We never wipe profiles or alter your underlying storage drives without your explicit, written consent.

Cybersecurity & Windows Environments

I think I have allowed scammers onto my machine — can you save it?

Yes, immediately. We run a priority Scam Recovery Service to secure compromised computers. We cleanly trace and remove dangerous Remote Access Tools (RATs) that fraudsters use to monitor displays, clear browser extension backdoors, scan for malicious files, and supply an official technical engineering report if required by your bank's fraud department.

Can you help prepare my old machine for the Windows 10 End of Support?

Absolutely. Microsoft has officially terminated standard lifecycle security updates for Windows 10, leaving unupgraded systems vulnerable to web threats. We can execute a full hardware compatibility check on your setup. If your machine fails standard Windows 11 compliance, we can often deploy minor component modifications or memory upgrades to transition your current laptop or desktop safely, saving you the high cost of buying a completely new computer.
