We can repair a loose or broken hinge, and the sooner you bring it in, the less there is to fix. A loose hinge feels like a small thing until the lid starts dropping to one side. Once one side gives, the plastic around the screws cracks, the bezel (the frame around the screen) twists, and the display cable that runs through the hinge can get stretched or pinched every time you open it. That's how a hinge repair can turn into a [cracked screen](/blog/cracked-laptop-screen-wickford-essex/) as well.

## Three things to avoid with a loose hinge until we've seen it

- **Rest it rather than using it "carefully".** Each open and close wears down the plastic that's still holding.
- **Leave the lid free rather than taping or propping it in place.** Tape can hide a display cable that's already tearing.
- **Move the lid gently and stop at the crack rather than forcing it.** That's how the screen panel shatters, and it can tear the cables that run through the hinge.

When you get in touch, please send a couple of photos of the laptop as it is now, without opening or moving the lid any further. If you've already noticed lines, flicker or a dark patch during normal use, tell us that too, because it suggests the display cable may be involved.

## Why cheap lids fail in a school bag

A 14-inch plastic case with a heavy screen, packed into a rucksack daily, puts a lot of strain on two small corners. Once one screw post cracks, the other hinge takes all the force and tends to follow.

Aluminium laptops can fail differently: the screw thread can strip, or the hinge can stiffen until it cracks the glass. Either way, we'd rather see it before the cable goes.
