## Why two cracked laptops aren't the same total

The labour floor barely moves. The panel does, for ordinary reasons:

- **The model.** A school Chromebook and a 15" touchscreen aren't the same part.
- **Touch or not.** A touch layer is a different assembly to a plain LCD.
- **What else broke.** Lines, flicker, or a dim picture can be the panel, or a display cable in the hinge. We'd rather find a cable than sell you glass you don't need. A twisted lid can be a hinge as well as a panel.
- **The machine around the screen.** A cracked lid on a laptop that also won't charge is two jobs. Screen labour doesn't include a battery or a DC jack.

That's why a neighbour's invoice isn't your quote. Diagnostics are free so the split is clear before you agree.

Your files almost always stay where they are. A panel swap doesn't wipe the drive. If the lid shows nothing, an external monitor tells us whether the rest of the machine is healthy before anyone pays for glass.
