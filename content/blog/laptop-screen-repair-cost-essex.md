## Labour and the panel are separate lines

The £99 is the work: stripping the bezel, moving the hinges carefully, fitting the matching panel, and testing the lid through a full open and close. It is a **from £99** labour floor for Windows laptops and Chromebooks.

The second line is the panel itself. I price that from the trade invoice for your model, and you see it before it is ordered. A basic HD panel and a touchscreen assembly do not cost the same, so I will not print a table of “Retina” or OLED prices that pretends every lid is identical. If I do not have your exact part number, the honest answer is “I will quote it”, not a made-up band.

MacBook lids are a different assembly. Fitting labour for those is listed on the [Wickford screen page](/laptop-screen-repair-wickford/) at **£85 plus the panel**, which is why an Apple quote is not the Windows £99 line with a different logo.

Most jobs are ready in **1 to 3 working days**, set by how fast the UK supplier can ship that panel. I would rather tell you the wait than promise a same-day glass I do not have on the shelf.

## Why two cracked laptops are not the same total

The labour floor barely moves. The panel does, for boring reasons:

- **The model.** A school Chromebook and a 15" touchscreen are not the same part.
- **Touch or not.** A touch layer is a different assembly to a plain LCD.
- **What else broke.** Lines, flicker, or a dim picture can be the panel, or a display cable in the hinge. I would rather find a cable than sell you glass you do not need. A twisted lid can be a hinge as well as a panel.
- **The machine around the screen.** A cracked lid on a laptop that also will not charge is two jobs. Screen labour does not include a battery or a DC jack.

That is why a neighbour’s invoice is not your quote. Diagnostics are free so the split is clear before you agree.

Your files almost always stay where they are. A panel swap does not wipe the drive. If the lid shows nothing, an external monitor tells us whether the rest of the machine is healthy before anyone pays for glass.
