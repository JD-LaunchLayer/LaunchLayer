Data recovery exists because backups did not. I would rather you never need the [Wickford data recovery page](/data-recovery). A backup is boring, cheap, and the only reason a dead laptop is an inconvenience instead of a family argument.

This is not [digital inheritance](/blog/secure-digital-inheritance-guide) (who gets the photos when you are gone). This is Tuesday: the disk dies, the laptop is stolen from the C2C, or a nasty PDF locks the Documents folder.

## The only rule that matters: 3-2-1, in English

- **3 copies** of anything you would cry about (the live files, plus two backups).
- **2 different types of media** — for example the PC plus a portable SSD, not two folders on the same disk.
- **1 copy off-site** — a cloud account you control, or a drive that lives at work / a relative’s house, not in the same kitchen as the laptop.

One USB stick left plugged in 24/7 is not a backup. Ransomware encrypts that too. A “backup” folder on D: of the same PC is also not a backup.

## What to copy first (in this order)

1. **Photos and WhatsApp / iPhone exports.** People think Word documents; they mean 14 years of children.
2. **Tax, house, and school PDFs.**
3. **Outlook PST / mail archives** if you still keep mail on the PC. (If you are still on @gmail.com for a business, read the [professional email post](/blog/professional-business-email-setup-essex) as well.)
4. **Browser bookmarks and password manager export** — or, better, a manager that already syncs.
5. Programmes can be reinstalled. Licences and installers are nice; they are not the emergency.

If the machine is already slow or clicking, **do not** run a full backup overnight and hope. Image the disk on the bench. See [data recovery](/data-recovery) and stop using the PC.

## Cloud is fine. “A folder called Backup on the Desktop” is not.

OneDrive, Google Drive and iCloud are excellent **if they are actually signed in and actually syncing**. I see Wickford laptops every month where OneDrive shows a little cloud icon and the important folder was set to “online only” on a machine that never finished the upload.

Open the cloud website from your phone. If last week’s photos are not there, they are not backed up.

A cheap 1TB portable SSD that you plug in once a week is still the most reliable local copy I recommend for people who do not want a subscription. Label it. Put a reminder in the calendar. Unplug it when you are done.

Windows File History and a Mac Time Machine drive are fine for versioned copies of Documents. They are not a substitute for checking the cloud website. I would rather you had a slightly messy 3-2-1 than a beautiful app you never opened.

## Small businesses in SS11 / SS12

If invoices live in one person’s Documents folder, you do not have a business backup. You have a single point of failure with a nice logo. The [solopreneur IT post](/blog/it-support-for-solopreneurs-wickford-essex) is the wider picture; this page is the disk. Compliance theatre without a restore test is how you discover the backup was empty the week after a scare — the [small business compliance note](/blog/is-your-small-business-compliant) is the awkward cousin of this guide.

I can set a simple weekly image plus cloud for a one- or two-person Wickford firm without turning it into an enterprise project. That is cheaper than pretending locked files only happen to other counties. If you already have a ransom note, stop copying files around and use [Wickford virus removal](/wickford-virus-removal) first.

## What a workshop backup looks like vs DIY

DIY is a portable SSD and a cloud account you control. A workshop job is an image of the whole disk *before* we clone onto an [SSD](/blog/laptop-ssd-upgrade-wickford-essex) or chase a [blue screen](/blog/windows-blue-screen-fix-essex). Different tools, same idea: a copy that does not live only inside the sick machine.

If the laptop also will not hold a charge, bring the brick — that is the [charging guide](/blog/laptop-not-charging-wickford-essex), not a backup problem. If it overheats every time File History runs, fix the dust first ([overheating](/blog/fix-overheating-laptop-wickford-essex)).
