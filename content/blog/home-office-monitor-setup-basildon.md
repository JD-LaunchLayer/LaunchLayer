A kitchen table and a 14" laptop is a commute, not a desk. I set up a lot of Basildon, Laindon and Wickford spare-bedrooms: one monitor, a dock, a keyboard, and a laptop that no longer overheats in anyone’s lap.

This is not another [Wi-Fi dead zone article](/blog/resolving-wi-fi-dead-zones-in-basildon). If Teams still tiles when you sit at the new desk, the radio may still be the villain — but start with the metal and the ports, because a bad dock looks exactly like “the internet is rubbish”.

## USB-C docks are not all the same brick

The laptop must **accept** power, video and data on that port. A phone hub that “has HDMI” will not run a 27" screen and charge a 90W Lenovo at once. The machine then slowly drains, throttles, and drops the monitor when you join a call.

Match:

- **Wattage** — if the original brick is 65W, the dock should supply at least that.
- **DisplayLink vs native DisplayPort alt-mode** — some older office laptops need DisplayLink; some hate it. I would rather test than guess from an Amazon title.
- **One cable** if you actually want one cable. Two dongles and a hope is how USB ports die.

If the laptop only charges from a barrel plug, you need a dock **plus** the original charger, or a USB-C board repair — see [not charging](/blog/laptop-not-charging-wickford-essex).

A dock that gets too hot to touch is underspecced or stuffed in a closed cabinet. Give it air. Same rule as the laptop.

## The monitor: 1080p is still fine

You do not need a 4K panel for Word and Outlook. You need a height-adjustable 24–27" 1080p or 1440p screen, an HDMI or DisplayPort cable that actually fits the dock, and night-time brightness that does not wreck your sleep. I will not upsell a curved gaming monitor for a bookkeeper in Laindon.

If the laptop lid is cracked, fix that first. A [screen replacement](/blog/cracked-laptop-screen-wickford-essex) plus an external monitor is a common combo: lid for travel, panel on the desk. Wickford panel labour is on the [screen repair page](/laptop-screen-repair-wickford).

Two monitors is fine if the dock and GPU can drive them. Three plus a 4K TV “for the news” is how you buy a crash in a driver. Start with one good panel.

## Heat and the closed-lid myth

Running closed-lid on a cushion is how you get [fan noise and thermal throttling](/blog/laptop-fan-noise-fix-essex). A dock and a stand that lets the underside breathe is part of the setup, not a nice-to-have. Dusty Basildon towers next to radiators get the [thermal service](/blog/pc-dust-thermal-servicing-basildon).

If the machine is still a spinning disk, the dock will not make Outlook fast. That is an [SSD](/blog/laptop-ssd-upgrade-wickford-essex). If Windows 11 was forced onto 8GB and a platter, read the [upgrade help](/blog/windows-11-upgrade-help-wickford) before you buy a 32" screen.
