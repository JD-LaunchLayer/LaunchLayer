## USB-C docks aren't all the same

The laptop's USB-C port has to **accept** power, video and data. A small phone-style hub that "has HDMI" may not be able to run a 27" screen and charge a 90W Lenovo at the same time. An underpowered dock can drain the battery, slow the laptop, or drop the monitor mid-call.

Check:

- **Wattage.** If the original charger is 65W, the dock should supply at least that.
- **DisplayLink vs native DisplayPort alt-mode.** Two ways of sending video over USB. Some older office laptops need DisplayLink, and some don't get on with it, so we'd rather test the dock than guess from a listing title.
- **One cable,** if you actually want one cable. A chain of adapters is less reliable than one compatible dock.

If the laptop only charges from a barrel plug, the dock won't power it, so keep the original charger plugged in.

A dock that's too hot to touch may be underpowered for the job, or it may just be shut in a closed cabinet. Give it air.

## The monitor: 1080p is still fine

You don't need a 4K panel for Word and Outlook. A height-adjustable 24–27" screen at 1080p or 1440p is plenty, with an HDMI or DisplayPort cable that actually fits the dock. A curved gaming monitor isn't the right buy for a bookkeeper in Laindon.

If the laptop lid is cracked, fix that first. A [screen replacement](/blog/cracked-laptop-screen-wickford-essex/) plus an external monitor is a common pair: the lid for travel, the bigger screen on the desk. Wickford panel labour is on the [screen repair page](/laptop-screen-repair-wickford/).

Two monitors are fine if the dock and the laptop can both drive them. Start with one good screen and add more once that's stable.

## Heat, and the closed-lid myth

Running a laptop closed-lid on a cushion is a common route to [fan noise and thermal throttling](/blog/laptop-fan-noise-fix-essex/), where the laptop slows itself down to stay cool. A stand that lets the underside breathe helps. Dusty towers next to radiators get the [thermal service](/blog/pc-dust-thermal-servicing-basildon/).

If the machine still has a spinning hard disk, a dock won't make Outlook faster, but an [SSD](/blog/laptop-ssd-upgrade-wickford-essex/) usually will. And if Windows 11 went onto 8GB of RAM and a hard drive, read the [upgrade help](/blog/windows-11-upgrade-help-wickford/) before buying a bigger screen.
