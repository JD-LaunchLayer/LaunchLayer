## USB-C docks aren't all the same

The laptop has to **accept** power, video and data on that port. A phone hub that "has HDMI" won't run a 27" screen and charge a 90W Lenovo at once. The machine then slowly drains, throttles, and drops the monitor when you join a call.

Match:

- **Wattage.** If the original brick is 65W, the dock should supply at least that.
- **DisplayLink vs native DisplayPort alt-mode.** Some older office laptops need DisplayLink. Some hate it. We'd rather test the dock than guess from a listing title.
- **One cable,** if you actually want one cable. Two dongles and a hope is how USB ports die.

If the laptop only charges from a barrel plug, the dock won't power it. Keep the original charger plugged in as well.

A dock that's too hot to touch is underspecified, or it's stuffed in a closed cabinet. Give it air. Same rule as the laptop.

## The monitor: 1080p is still fine

You don't need a 4K panel for Word and Outlook. A height-adjustable 24–27" 1080p or 1440p screen is enough, with an HDMI or DisplayPort cable that actually fits the dock, and a night-time brightness that doesn't wreck your sleep. A curved gaming monitor isn't the right buy for a bookkeeper in Laindon.

If the laptop lid is cracked, fix that first. A [screen replacement](/blog/cracked-laptop-screen-wickford-essex/) plus an external monitor is a common pair: the lid for travel, the panel on the desk. Wickford panel labour is on the [screen repair page](/laptop-screen-repair-wickford/).

Two monitors is fine if the dock and the laptop can drive them. Three, plus a TV, is how a driver starts crashing. Start with one good panel.

## Heat, and the closed-lid myth

Running closed-lid on a cushion is how you get [fan noise and thermal throttling](/blog/laptop-fan-noise-fix-essex/). A dock and a stand that lets the underside breathe is part of the setup. Dusty towers next to radiators get the [thermal service](/blog/pc-dust-thermal-servicing-basildon/).

If the machine still has a spinning disk, the dock won't make Outlook fast. That's an [SSD](/blog/laptop-ssd-upgrade-wickford-essex/). If Windows 11 was put onto 8GB and a hard drive, read the [upgrade help](/blog/windows-11-upgrade-help-wickford/) before you buy a bigger screen.
