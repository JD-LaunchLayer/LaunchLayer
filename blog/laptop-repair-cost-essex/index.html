How Much Does a Laptop Repair Cost in Essex? 2026 Price Guide

Useful Tips 14 Oct The LaunchLayer workshop

People ask what a laptop repair cost in Essex actually is, then find a forum thread full of guesses. Here’s the list we quote from in Wickford. These are our normal starting prices, the same as on our services and pricing page.

The order of the job is the same wherever you live. Diagnostics are free. We find the fault, and then you get a fixed quote before any work starts or any part is ordered. If a repair isn’t worth doing, you don’t pay labour for the bad news. ‘From’ is where the labour starts. We price your part separately.

That’s us at LaunchLayer, in Wickford. Call 07367 652987 and we’ll book a drop-off, or you can ask about collection. Collection and return is free in Wickford SS11 and SS12. Elsewhere in South Essex, please ask.

Starting prices

  • Diagnostics are free.
  • Hardware and general repair starts from £49.
  • Battery labour starts from £49 plus the part, and the battery itself is quoted for your model before it’s ordered.
  • A DC jack or charging port is a flat £65 where the port is removable or sits on its own small board. Ports soldered to the main board are priced separately, quoted after diagnosis. The plugged in not charging guide helps you narrow down whether it’s the charger, the cable or the port.
  • Windows and Chromebook screen fitting is £50 plus the panel. MacBook screen fitting is £85 plus the panel, and Mac panels usually cost more. The panel is a separate line, because a Chromebook panel and a touchscreen panel aren’t the same part. How that quote is built is explained on laptop screen repair Wickford.
  • Hinge and lid work starts from £75.
  • Keyboard labour starts from £55 plus the part.
  • SSD upgrade £65 + drive (includes fitting and data transfer). RAM, where it can be upgraded, starts from £49 plus the part. Much RAM is soldered.
  • MacBook repairs start from £85 plus the parts or the panel.
  • The MOT is £60. That’s a fixed health check, separate from the £45 software fix and from any hardware repair.
  • A general software fix is £45 for up to 60 minutes.
  • Virus, malware and scam removal is a fixed £69, as on services and pricing. It’s separate from the software fix, and it doesn’t cover ransomware data recovery.
  • A fresh Windows install is £59.
  • A Windows 11 upgrade starts from £69.
  • Liquid damage starts from £95.
  • An insurance damage report is £40.
  • Outside SS11/SS12, home visits are priced by location; we’ll confirm the cost before booking.

Data recovery is quoted on its own, after we’ve assessed the drive. The full list is on services and pricing.

If the laptop also won’t switch on, start with the won’t turn on guide, so we aren’t pricing a screen for a machine that has a separate power fault.

What “from” means on the invoice

“From £49” means the labour starts there, after a free diagnostic. It doesn’t mean every laptop leaves at £49. Keyboard labour still starts from £55, and a dust clean stays £55. A loose hinge, a port and a worn battery are three separate lines, so you can see what each one costs.

You approve the quote before we order a part. If the part arrives and the fault turns out to be somewhere else, we’ll explain that before you pay for any work you didn’t agree to. If we can’t do the repair, you don’t pay labour.
