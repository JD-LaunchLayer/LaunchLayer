Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

What happens to the personal files left on my laptop?

As soon as we receive your device, it undergoes military-grade, block-level data erasure. We guarantee 100% destruction of all personal files, passwords, and records before the hardware is ever refurbished or dismantled.

Do you accept computers that are completely dead or broken?

Yes. Even if a machine won't turn on or has a shattered screen, we want it. We can salvage the internal components to repair other donated machines, ensuring nothing goes to a landfill unnecessarily.

Do you take chargers, cables, or old peripherals?

Absolutely. Power infrastructure like laptop chargers, power bricks, and loose cables are in high demand and are essential for getting these devices back up and running.

Is there any cost for me to donate my old technology?

None at all. Our data wiping, hardware repair, and the subsequent transport logistics managed by Boxers Barbers are completely free. You just need to drop the items off at our Wickford workshop.
