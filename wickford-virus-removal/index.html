Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

What should I do if I have already let a scammer onto my machine?

Immediately turn off your Wi-Fi router or disconnect the computer's network cable to block incoming commands. Do not restart or use the system to log into online banking or email accounts. Keep the device completely disconnected from the internet and have a specialist run a hardware system cleanup to check for hidden entry routes.

What are the common signs of an active malware infection?

The most immediate signs include sudden, extreme drops in computer performance, frequent system freezing, unprompted web browser redirects, and aggressive pop-up warnings. If your cooling fans suddenly start running loud when your device is sitting idle, background software may be executing processes without your knowledge.

Will I lose my family photos and documents during a cleanup?

No. We operate strictly under a "Data-First" protocol. Our workshop isolation tools allow us to clean and protect malicious files or operating system blocks while leaving your underlying documents, files, folders, and photos completely intact and untouched.
