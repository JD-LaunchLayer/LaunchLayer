Can You Make a Sluggish Old Computer Fast Again?

In the vast majority of cases, yes. A slow laptop is rarely ready for the scrap heap; it is usually just held back by a cluttered operating system or an aging mechanical drive. Moving your system to a high-speed Solid State Drive (SSD) can make an 8-year-old machine run faster than a brand-new budget laptop on the high street. Laptop screen repair in Brentwood

Not every “broken screen” is the panel — and the fix depends on which fault you’ve actually got.

Free collection across CM13, CM14, and CM15; free diagnostic before we quote.

Which fault do you actually have?

Cracked or shattered glass

Visible damage or spidering cracks. Usually a genuine screen replacement — the most straightforward fix.

Flickering, lines, or colour bleed

Image present but distorted. Often the panel, sometimes a loose display cable (much cheaper) — our diagnostic tells the difference.

Completely black, but the laptop is on

Fans spinning, keyboard lit, but no image. Frequently not the screen — could be the graphics chip, backlight, or cable.

Why quotes vary by panel type

We price by the panel you need, not a flat guess: standard HD/FHD (most everyday Windows laptops), high-resolution / 4K, touchscreen / 2-in-1, and MacBook Retina. You get a fixed quote after the free diagnostic — no labour-from figures shown here.

Spilled something on a laptop or MacBook?

Tea, coffee, or water — the next few minutes matter more than anything we do afterwards.

Free collection across CM13–CM15; call and say it’s a fresh spill if you need priority pickup.

Do this first

Step 01

Cut the power immediately

Hold the power button until it fully shuts down.

Step 02

Unplug the charger and remove the battery if you can

Speed matters more than anything else.

Step 03

Don’t turn it on to “check”

That’s what usually turns a repairable spill into a written-off logic board.

Step 04

Stand it liquid-side-down and leave it

Rice doesn’t reach internal components — it’s a myth.

What we do when it arrives

We open the machine and inspect the logic board, keyboard membrane, and battery for corrosion under magnification — not just wipe the outside. Some spills, caught fast, are a straightforward clean and dry-out. Others need component-level repair, or occasionally aren’t economical to fix. You’ll get a straight answer and a fixed quote before anything chargeable.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you book.

Do you charge for PC or laptop collection in Brentwood?

No. We offer a completely free collection and return delivery service for all approved computer repair and diagnostic jobs throughout the Brentwood area, ensuring you do not have to struggle with town centre parking queues.

Can you fix a laptop that is running very slowly?

In most cases, yes. Severe slowdown is usually caused by an aging internal mechanical hard drive or cluttered background software processes. We specialise in targeted system tuning and SSD upgrades.

What areas around Brentwood do you collect from?

We provide regular free collection and return delivery loops across all Brentwood postcodes (CM13, CM14 & CM15), including Hutton, Warley, and Shenfield. A lot of CM13–CM15 customers are A129/A127 commuters who prefer dropping a machine at the Wickford bench rather than paying high-street repair-shop premiums in Brentwood or further afield.

Will I lose my files during a repair or upgrade?

No. We run strict "Data-First" protocols. We protect your family documents, bookmarks, and local records as an absolute priority, ensuring nothing is wiped unnecessarily.

Why doesn’t screen repair have one flat rate?

Panel cost varies — a standard HD panel is far cheaper than a 4K or touchscreen one. A flat rate would overcharge simple jobs or undercharge complex ones.

Screen is black but the laptop turns on — is that a screen repair?

Not always. It could be the panel, backlight, display cable, or graphics chip. Our free diagnostic identifies which.

How long does a screen replacement take?

Often same-day to next-day if we have the panel in stock. Less common panels may take 1–3 days while we source the exact part.

It still turns on after a spill — is it damaged?

Possibly. Corrosion can develop over hours or days after a spill. If it’s still on, power it off now rather than waiting to see what happens.

Is sugary liquid worse than water?

Yes — sugar and salt residue accelerates corrosion even when everything looks dry on the surface. The sooner it’s cleaned professionally, the better the odds.

Can you collect same-day from Brentwood for a fresh spill?

Call us and say it’s a fresh spill — we prioritise these ahead of routine bookings across CM13, CM14, and CM15.

My MacBook is out of AppleCare — does that limit what you can do?

Once AppleCare has expired there’s no warranty left to void, so an independent repair is often faster and cheaper — especially on older models Apple no longer actively supports.

What MacBook parts do you use?

Genuine “pulled” parts from donor MacBooks, or strict OEM-equivalent components when those aren’t available. We’ll always tell you which before work starts.
